# PowerLeaders Chama: Rules and Information

## About the chama
- Name: PowerLeaders Chama, based in Thika, started in 2019, with 25 members.
- Officials: Chairperson, Secretary and Treasurer, elected every 2 years.

## Monthly contributions
- Each member contributes KES 2,000 per month, due by the 5th of the month.
- Pay by M-Pesa to the chama paybill (the treasurer gives the number) or in cash to the treasurer at the meeting.
- Merry-go-round: each month one member receives the pot of KES 10,000, following the rotation list kept by the secretary.

## Welfare
- When a member or an immediate family member (spouse, child or parent) passes away, every member contributes KES 500 to the welfare fund.
- The welfare fund pays the bereaved member KES 20,000.

## Meetings
- Monthly meeting on the first Saturday of the month at 2:00 pm, at the home of the member whose turn it is (rotation list kept by the secretary).
- The meeting starts promptly at 2:00 pm. Anyone arriving after 2:15 pm is late.
- Quorum: 13 members.
- Annual general meeting: first Saturday of December.

## Fines
- Late monthly contribution (paid after the 5th): KES 200.
- Late to a meeting (after 2:15 pm): KES 100.
- Absent without sending an apology to the secretary before the meeting: KES 300.
- Absent with an apology sent beforehand: no fine.
- Fines are paid at the next meeting.

## Loans
- A member can apply after 6 months of membership, with no more than 1 late contribution in the last 6 months.
- The maximum loan is 3 times the member's total savings.
- Interest is 10% flat, and the loan must be repaid within 3 months.
- The member needs one guarantor, who must be another member.
- Apply to the treasurer. The committee decides at the monthly meeting.

## Joining and leaving
- Joining fee: KES 1,000, plus approval by the committee.
- A new member pays the first monthly contribution when joining.
- A member who wants to leave must give written notice to the chairperson one month in advance.

## Who to ask
- Questions about balances, payments made or loan status: the treasurer.
- Questions about rules, disputes or decisions: the chairperson.
- Meeting rotation and apologies: the secretary.
